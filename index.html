Usage information

Through Google Analytics for Firebase we record which screens you open and which features you finish — for example that a breathing session was completed, or that a check-in was saved. These events record that something happened, never what you wrote. We use this to find where the app is confusing or broken.

Firebase also collects standard technical information: a device identifier, device model, operating system version, approximate region, and crash reports.

Subscription information

If you subscribe, we store your subscription status, the plan you chose, and when it renews or expires. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, with RevenueCat verifying the purchase on our behalf.

We never see your card details. Releva has no access to your card number, bank account, or billing address. Those stay between you and Google Play.

What we do not collect

  • Precise location. Releva asks for a rough region from your device settings only to show the right crisis helpline numbers.
  • Contacts, photos, files, or your microphone, except where you explicitly use voice input and your device supports it.
  • Any record of whether you opened a crisis screen or called a helpline. That is deliberately not logged.

3. Using Releva without an account

You can explore Releva as a guest. A guest session still creates an anonymous identifier so your entries save to your device's session, but it holds no email or name.

If you later create an account, your guest data carries over to it. If you never create an account, that data stays tied to the anonymous session and is lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data — we have no way to recover it, because we have no way to identify you.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is held in Google Cloud Firestore in the asia-southeast1 region (Singapore), on infrastructure operated by Google. It is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Access rules are enforced per account: each account can read and write only its own records. These rules are applied by Firestore itself, not by the app, so they hold even if the app is modified.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, your data is transferred outside your region. Google Cloud provides Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

6. Advertising

Premium removes all advertising. On the free plan, ads may appear on browsing screens such as Explore, Progress and Profile.

Ads never appear on crisis support screens, during a breathing, grounding, focus or sleep session, in journal or check-in flows, in conversations, during onboarding, or on any purchase screen.

Advertising is never targeted using anything you write or record. Advertisers receive no access to your entries, check-ins, moods or preferences.

Personalised advertising is switched off by default. It stays off unless you are 16 or over and have given consent. If an age is not provided, we treat the user as under 16.

7. How long we keep things

  • While your account is open: your entries and check-ins stay until you delete them.
  • If you delete your account: your account and its records are removed from our active systems within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.
  • Analytics: retained for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Payment records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years. These contain no journal or health content.

8. Your rights

Whatever country you are in, you can do the following:

  • See your data. Everything you have recorded is visible in the app.
  • Export it. Profile › Your data › Export.
  • Correct it. Edit your profile and preferences at any time.
  • Delete it. Profile › Your data › Delete account. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn off analytics or personalised ads in Profile › Privacy.

If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR also gives you the right to restrict or object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to your national data protection authority. Our lawful bases are: performing our contract with you (running the app), your consent (analytics, personalised ads, and the processing of health-related information you enter), and our legitimate interests (keeping the service secure).

If you are in California, the CCPA gives you the right to know what is collected, to delete it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

To make any request, write to hamzasiddique951@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
